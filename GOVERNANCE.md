# Governance

The human owner defines tasks, capabilities, resources, spending limits and
deployment authority. Models cannot grant themselves access. COC reports actual
execution receipts separately from generated explanations and interpretations.

All seven technology areas remain in scope. An unavailable provider or optional
platform does not remove an implemented kernel or block unrelated work.
Implementation status must distinguish PASS, FAIL, SKIPPED, BLOCKED and NOT TESTED.

The repair laboratory is isolated development work. A verified correction is a
delivery candidate, never authorization to deploy into an application or clinical
environment. Rollback of external effects requires an application-specific
transaction/compensation contract.

Publications are prereleases until their acceptance criteria and operational
assurance are met. Publish only scanned source and verified artifacts. Do not
include private credentials, runtime databases, machine configuration or borrowed
reference project binaries. No DRAGONHYDRA change is authorized by this project.

The current direct OpenAI test budget is exhausted: three attempted inference
requests, no successful inference, quota/billing block. Do not reset or replace the
ledger, switch credentials, or schedule retries without a new owner authorization.
